FAQ

Questions

Do I need Excel?

No. The file opens in Google Sheets (free), Apple Numbers (free on Apple devices) or LibreOffice (free). Upload it to Google Drive and choose "Open with Google Sheets" to use it on your phone.

I'm not a spreadsheet person. Is it hard?

You only type in the cream-colored cells. Dropdowns handle clients and categories, and a Start Here tab walks you through setup in five short steps. Everything else calculates itself.

Does it handle quarterly taxes?

Yes. The Tax tab adds up your set-aside for each tax period, tracks what you've paid and shows due dates with a status (Paid, Due in X days, Overdue). It defaults to the US quarterly schedule, and you can change the periods and dates to match your country. It's a savings tracker, not tax advice.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. Pick any currency symbol, and set the tax set-aside percentage that fits where you live. The set-aside is a savings estimate, not tax advice.

I'm not a freelancer. Is it for me?

If your income changes month to month (contractors, creators, consultants, gig workers, small-business owners, commission sales), Steady was built for you. Salaried side-hustlers use it to track the side income separately.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. Your financial data never leaves your file. Many people find a weekly two-minute log also makes them more aware of their spending.

How do I get the file?

Right after checkout you'll get an email with a download link (a .zip containing the spreadsheet and the demo file). You can download it again any time from that email.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 30 days for a full refund. No questions asked.
